different variables.

* Does genetic variation in *ATP2B4* affect its expression levels?  (Go [here](./README.md).)

* Do ABO blood groups protect against malaria?  (Go [here](./abo/README.md).)

* How effective are malaria vaccines?  (Go [here](./vaccine/README.md).)
